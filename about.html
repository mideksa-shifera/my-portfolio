About Me

My name is Mideksa Shifera, and I was born and raised in Sululta Town, in the beautiful Oromia Region of Ethiopia. Growing up in Sululta, I developed a strong appreciation for learning and community values. I completed my education up to Grade 12 in my hometown, where I excelled academically and cultivated essential skills such as discipline, curiosity, and perseverance.

I am currently a third-year Computer Science student at Arsi University, where I am expanding my knowledge in programming, algorithms, software development, and modern web technologies. My studies have equipped me with both the theoretical understanding and practical experience necessary to build efficient, scalable, and user-friendly digital solutions. I am particularly passionate about exploring emerging technologies and applying them to real-world problems.

As a website developer, I specialize in building responsive and dynamic websites. My current project involves developing a full-stack website, where I integrate front-end design, back-end functionality, and database management to create complete web applications. This project has helped me enhance my skills in HTML, CSS, JavaScript, PHP, and database systems, while also teaching me the importance of design thinking, user experience, and problem-solving.

Beyond my academic and professional work, I am also a content creator on YouTube, where I post football editing videos. Through this platform, I have learned to combine creativity, technical editing skills, and audience engagement strategies. It has also taught me how to manage a project from concept to completion and the importance of consistency and quality in any endeavor.

I am also a licensed driver, which reflects my responsibility, focus, and ability to manage tasks safely and effectively. My hobbies include exploring technology trends, experimenting with new software tools, and following sports and digital media content. These interests allow me to continually grow my creativity, technical knowledge, and awareness of current industry trends.

I am driven by curiosity, ambition, and a desire to make a meaningful impact. I aim to combine my academic knowledge, technical skills, and creative abilities to develop projects that not only showcase innovation but also provide real value to users and communities. I believe in lifelong learning and strive to challenge myself constantly, whether through building websites, creating engaging content, or pursuing new opportunities for growth.

Ultimately, my goal is to become a well-rounded technology professional who can contribute positively to the digital world, while continuing to pursue my passions in web development, content creation, and innovation. I am excited about the opportunities that lie ahead and am committed to developing both personally and professionally as I move forward in my career.
